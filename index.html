welcome to the grid

Two grids. One interference pattern.

Grid A runs 16 steps. Grid B runs at a different length — creating rhythmic interference like two waves crossing. Paint cells, choose a mode, and let the Markov chain evolve.

Below the grids, walk Miffy through the world. Your footsteps paint cells. Jump to accent. Stand still to erode. The zone you're in determines which instrument you paint.

WalkArrow keys move Miffy and paint grid cells in the zone's color.
Jump↑ to jump. Landing stamps a full-probability accent cell.
ErodeHold still — Miffy scrubs nearby cells, creating silence.
